PUREX Storage Tunnels

218-E-14 “Tunnel 1” and 218-E-15 “Tunnel 2” · Hanford Site, 200 East Area · as they stood circa 1999

This is a historical simulation built from public sources: the RCRA permit application, DOE engineering evaluations, construction-era photographs, and as-built drawing excerpts. It depicts the tunnels as they stood around 1999, loaded to their final contents, sealed, and standing.

No person ever stood inside these tunnels once the railcars were in. Interior radiation exceeded 5 roentgen per hour from the first car onward, and no photograph of the loaded interiors exists. In 2017 and 2019 both tunnels were filled solid with engineered grout. The walk was impossible while they stood and is impossible now.

Where the record is silent, this simulation infers period-plausible detail. Inferred details are labeled inferred. Placards quote their sources. The visitor’s headlamp, the ability to pass the sealed doors, and the inspection view are presentation conceits, not history.

This is not site information and is not a substitute for any Hanford document, procedure, or training. Photo credits: U.S. Department of Energy.
