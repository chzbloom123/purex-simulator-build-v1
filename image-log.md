# PUREX Storage Tunnels — Image Log
Research date: 2026-09-06. Compiled for historically-accurate 3D simulator reference.
Trimmed 2026-09-06 to the 11 images kept for the project; source and license information retained for each.

## License framework used
- **PD-USGov (DOE)**: Works of U.S. federal employees published on energy.gov / hanford.gov / content.govdelivery.com (USDOEOEM account) are public domain under 17 U.S.C. §105. energy.gov photo usage guidance: DOE photos may be used with credit ("Photo courtesy of the U.S. Department of Energy").
- **DOE/contractor public release**: CHPRC/CH2M HILL Plateau Remediation Company documents posted by DOE on hanford.gov (engineering evaluations, posters). Written by a DOE contractor under contract; publicly released by DOE without restriction. Historical photos embedded in them are 1950s-60s Atomic Energy Commission/DOE-site archive images. Treated as usable for reference; flag if redistributing commercially.
