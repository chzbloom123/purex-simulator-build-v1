[CONFIRMED] Worker protections: >3,000 workers sheltered in place (ANL-EPI); "Nearly 4,800 workers at the nuclear site were told to shelter indoors" (Seattle Times 2017-05-10 https://www.seattletimes.com/seattle-news/environment/hanford-declares-alert-emergency-evacuates-workers-because-of-problems-with-contaminated-tunnels/); FAA temporary flight restriction 10:35 a.m. (Statesman Journal); take-cover expanded site-wide incl. [CONFIRMED] Collapse location: "The hole opened up in the shorter tunnel near where it joins the longer tunnel." — DOE-EOC-2017 evening statement (via ABC News/NBC Right Now); Weather Channel/AP: "at the junction of two tunnels to the east of the facility"
